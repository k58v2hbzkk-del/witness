You saw it first.

You are at the match, in the rain, watching the speech. The market is a few seconds behind you. Witness keeps that moment's markets in the corner of your eye, and one gesture on your glasses backs what you just saw.

Try the replay matchHow it works
01

Pick the moment

A live game Polymarket lists, your city's weather markets, a show or a speech. Witness pulls its two-sided markets and streams the order book live.

02

Watch with your eyes

The glasses show the odds, the score and the clock. When something happens, a goal, a red card, the rain starting, Witness flags it and shows that the market has not moved yet.

03

One gesture

Scroll up to back YES or the home side, scroll down for NO. Tap moves between markets. Double-tap cancels while the order waits.

04

See if you were early

Witness watches the market after your tap and tells you whether it followed you, by how many points and how many seconds later. Receipts land in Telegram.

Real rules, no fake edge

Polymarket delays sports orders. Each sports market has a secondsDelay so people at the venue can't trade before everyone else sees the play. Witness paper orders wait out that same delay and fill at the ask at that moment, not the price you saw. If the market got there first, you pay for it, and Witness shows the difference.

Paper money only. Prices and scores are live public Polymarket data. Trades are simulated against them. No wallet, no keys, no custody. The replay match is simulated and always labelled REPLAY.

Your eyes, not a feed. Witness never trades on its own. Nothing happens until you gesture, and nothing is recorded: the glasses have no camera and Witness does not use the microphone.
